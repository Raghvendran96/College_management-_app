## 🔳 2. Flagship Module Analysis (Screen-by-Screen)

### 📈 Master Dashboard (Institutional Pulse)
*   **Announcement Ticker**: A live, animated news node at the top of the hub for real-time institutional alerts.
*   **Fiscal Revenue Hub**: A high-fidelity visual summary of tuition clearance and institutional revenue health.
*   **Task Node Management**: Quick-access widgets for pending audits and administrative directives.

### 🏛️ Academic Hub (Scholar Identity)
*   **Student Directory**: A centralized lattice for managing all scholar records.
*   **Digital ID Passport**: Interactive profile hub allowing students to upload **Real-Time Photos** and generate a printable institutional ID card with QR validation.
*   **Profile Audit**: Faculty can audit scholar nodes to verify enrollment and academic status.

### 🎓 Academic Merit Portal (Assignments & Grading)
*   **Scholar View**: Students see **"Only My Assignments"**—a private academic registry for submission and feedback tracking.
*   **Faculty Grading Lab**: Teachers audit submitted scholarly work using **Class & Scholar Filters**. 
*   **Document Audit**: The "Audit" button launches a high-fidelity institutional document review tab for faculty review.

### 💳 Fiscal Hub (Unified Payment Gateway)
*   **Payment Simulator**: Scholars can clear their financial audits via a high-end **Fee Payment Node** with QR (Scan & Pay) and Global Card options.
*   **Fee Lifecycle Tracking**: Real-time monitoring of "Pending," "Paid," and "Overdue" fiscal nodes.

### ⚙️ Settings Hub (Institutional Lattice & Go-Live)
*   **Lattice Manager**: Master Admins (`admin@college.edu`) can provision **Secondary Institutional Admins** for new colleges or groups.
*   **Longevity Analytics**: Master Admins track the **Total Usage Days** and node health of every managed institute in their network.
*   **'Go Live' Protocol**: The Definitive Handover action (detailed in Section 3).

## 🚀 3. Step-by-Step Handover (The "Go Live" Logic)
This protocol allows you to demonstrate the product in "Demo Mode" and then officially hand it over to a client as a pristine, blank environment.

### 🔹 Phase 1: Initiation
1.  Navigate to **Settings > System Danger Zone**.
2.  Click **Launch 'Go Live' Protocol**.

### 🔹 Phase 2: Secure Handover
1.  Enter the new permanent **Production Admin Email** and **Master Password**.
2.  Click **Finalize & Secure**. The system will perform a 2.5s "Institutional Purge."

### 🔹 Phase 3: The Result
*   **Data Purge**: Every simulated student, faculty, and finance record is permanently deleted.
*   **Guide Activation**: The system instantly **Opens the Seeding Manual (guide.pdf)** for the new admin.
*   **Clean State Launch**: The system redirects to the login screen. Upon the new admin's login, all modules will launch as **Zero-Byte registries**, ready for real production seeding.
